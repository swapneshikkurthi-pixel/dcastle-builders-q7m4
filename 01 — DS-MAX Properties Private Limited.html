DS-MAX Properties, founded in 2007 by Dr. K.V. Satish, grew from a marketing partner to a mid-market developer in Bengaluru. By 2024, they had 16 ongoing projects, which consolidated to 12 live projects by May 2026 with 89.26 lakh sq ft of saleable area. This year: In 2025-2026, DS-MAX launched 6 projects totaling 54.59 lakh sq ft in the 18 months ending March 2026.
