13.Their story, and this year

Founded in 1998 by B.S. Shivarama and R. Gopal Reddy, Concorde Group grew from a partnership to a private limited company by 2008. They traditionally relied on joint development agreements but are now shifting to outright land purchases. In FY26, they closed sales at ₹450 crore, handed over Concorde Auriga, and announced an ₹80 crore investment in Concorde Sienna. For FY27, they target ₹650 crore in sales and plan to deploy ₹200 crore for new projects, aiming to improve profit margins to 15%.

This year: In 2025-2026, Concorde missed its ₹525 crore sales target, closing at ₹450 crore due to approval delays that pushed two launches to FY27. They acquired the stressed Mayfair project via NCLT, launched Concorde Eleve, and invested ₹80 crore in a 3-acre North Bengaluru project (Sienna) featuring a 10,000 sq ft clubhouse.
